# The Steel Colossus Roadmap

Each stage pays for the next one. Nothing gets bought until the stage before it has earned its share.

Costs are rough 2026 estimates for a solo builder. Check them before committing to a stage.

| Stage | What you build | Rough cost | Skill it teaches | What it lets you sell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Gauntlet** ([plan](stage-one.md)) | Printed gauntlet with a gesture- and voice-fired palm repulsor; ADAM listening on the laptop | $380–430 | 3D printing, microcontrollers, Python voice control | Gauntlet STLs, printed parts, the build video series |
| **2. Helmet** | Motorized faceplate, LED eyes, an inside camera and display feeding ADAM, with fiberglass or carbon fiber reinforcement | $600–1,200 | CAD, servos, composites, embedded vision | Helmet STLs and kits, commissions, the first sponsorship pitch |
| **3. Chest and arc light** | Chest plate with a glowing arc reactor and a battery pack that powers the gauntlet and helmet | $800–1,500 | Power systems, battery safety, custom PCBs | Arc reactor kits, PCB designs (PCBWay or JLCPCB sponsorship) |
| **4. Full armor** | Complete wearable suit with metal plates cut by a service like SendCutSend over a printed and composite base | $5,000–15,000 | Metalwork, fit and articulation, systems integration | Convention appearances, paid commissions, a full documentary series |
| **5. Strength assist** | Powered exoskeleton joints on the legs and arms to carry the suit's weight | $10,000–50,000 | Actuators, control loops, safety engineering | Engineering consulting, open hardware, grants (Hackaday Prize and similar) |
| **6. Flight** | Jet or electric thrust | $100,000+ | Propulsion, aviation law | — |

## The rules

1. **Earn before you buy.** A stage starts when the last one's sales and sponsors cover at least half its cost.
2. **Film everything.** The audience is the funding engine from stage one onward.
3. **Each stage must work on its own.** A finished gauntlet beats an unfinished suit.
4. **No gambling, loans or credit cards** to speed things up. Stalling a stage is recoverable, but debt isn't.

## On flight

Real jet suits exist, such as Gravity Industries', but they cost hundreds of thousands, take years of training, and fall under FAA rules. Flight is the stage the other five pay for, not one to rush. By stage five you'll know whether it's still the goal, or whether the Colossus becomes something stronger on the ground.
